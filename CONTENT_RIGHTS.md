# Content rights and portfolio notices

This document explains how rights should be described for the mixed material shown in `looksawful.ru`. It supplements the root `LICENSE`; it does not replace a contract, release, upstream license or project-specific permission.

## Core rule

Portfolio display, authorship, copyright ownership and authority to sublicense are separate questions.

A public file, credit, screenshot, CDN URL, Git history entry or portfolio role does not by itself prove that Ivan Krushinsky owns every right embodied in the material or may license it to third parties.

Where rights are unknown or unverified, the public-safe position is: **no redistribution or sublicensing clearance is claimed**.

Model releases, consent records and private personal data should not be published in the repository.

## Internal media metadata: `reusable`

The current media schema contains a boolean field named `reusable`. That name is an internal content-management concept and **must not be interpreted as public permission for legal reuse**.

Until the schema is renamed and separated from rights metadata:

- `reusable: true` means only reusable within the portfolio/content system;
- it does not mean downloadable, sublicensable, open-source or externally reusable;
- it does not prove ownership or publication clearance;
- legal decisions must use the rights classification and evidence process in `docs/RIGHTS_AND_PROVENANCE.md`.

A future rights-aware schema should separate at least: `rightsClass`, `copyrightHolder`, `sourceUrl`, `licenseRef`, `portfolioDisplayBasis`, `sublicensingAuthority`, `attributionRequired`, `modelReleaseStatus`, `trademarkOwner`, `evidencePointer` and `lastVerifiedAt`.
