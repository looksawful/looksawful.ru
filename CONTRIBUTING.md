# Contributing

This repository is a proprietary, source-visible portfolio. It is not a general-purpose open-source collaboration project.

Unsolicited code, design, media, copy, data and other content contributions are not accepted unless Ivan Krushinsky expressly requests them.

Do not submit material unless you have authority to provide it and to grant every permission required by the applicable repository terms, platform terms and any expressly agreed contribution terms.

A contribution must not include confidential client material, personal data, third-party assets, copyrighted media, trademarks, model or performer material, copied code, course material or other protected content unless the contributor has a documented lawful basis for the submission and the repository owner has expressly agreed to accept it.

GitHub’s Terms of Service may independently govern content submitted through GitHub, including contributions to a repository containing a license. Those platform terms do not make third-party material safe to contribute and do not replace the need for contributor authority.

Security reports, factual corrections and requests concerning rights or attribution may still be raised through the repository’s ordinary contact or issue channels where appropriate. Submitting such a report does not grant a license to unrelated material.
